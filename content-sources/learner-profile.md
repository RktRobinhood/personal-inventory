# Source material — IB Learner Profile (aspirational module)

**Instrument id:** `learner-profile`
**Type:** Aspirational, **UNSCORED** (no scales, no bands). Composed of
info/teaching + select-and-commit + free-reflection sections (DESIGN). Framing =
**"full subversive, then constructive"**: each attribute opens with how you get
controlled/minimised if you lack it, then pivots to the empowered version + one
concrete move. Each reframe is **mapped back to its official IB attribute** so it
stays defensible to colleagues/parents.

## What is sourced vs authored (IMPORTANT)

- **SOURCED (the grounding framework):** the 10 official IB Learner Profile
  attributes and their official descriptors — published by the IBO. The reframes
  must map onto these exact attributes.
- **AUTHORED by the loop (not transcribed):** the subversive-then-constructive
  reframe copy and the commit/reflection prompts. This is aspirational
  pedagogical content (DESIGN explicitly assigns this to the loop), NOT
  psychometric items. ⇒ requires the **human subversive-voice / tone review**
  (Layer 2) before use — flagged in REVIEW.md.

## The 10 official attributes (names confirmed)

Descriptors marked ✓ are verbatim from the retrieved source; those marked
"(verify)" give the attribute only — the official descriptor must be confirmed
against the IB source before launch.

1. **Inquirers** — ✓ "IB learners nurture their curiosity, developing skills for inquiry and research."
2. **Knowledgeable** — ✓ (paraphrase retrieved) develop and use conceptual understanding across a range of disciplines. (verify exact official wording)
3. **Thinkers** — ✓ "using critical and creative thinking skills to analyse problems and make reasoned, ethical decisions."
4. **Communicators** — (verify) express themselves and collaborate effectively in more than one language and in many ways.
5. **Principled** — (verify) act with integrity, honesty, fairness, and a sense of responsibility.
6. **Open-minded** — (verify) appreciate their own and others' cultures and personal histories; seek and evaluate a range of perspectives.
7. **Caring** — (verify) show empathy, compassion, and respect; act to make a positive difference.
8. **Risk-takers (Courageous)** — ✓ "approach unfamiliar situations and uncertainty with courage and forethought… independence of spirit to explore new roles, ideas and strategies… brave and articulate in defending their beliefs."
9. **Balanced** — (verify) understand the importance of balancing intellectual, physical, and emotional aspects of life.
10. **Reflective** — (verify) thoughtfully consider the world and their own ideas and experience, and their strengths and limitations.
