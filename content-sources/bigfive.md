# Source material — Big Five (IPIP-NEO-120)

**Instrument id:** `bigfive`  
**Construct:** Five Factor Model — 5 domains x 6 facets x 4 items = 120 items.

## Provenance (public domain, cross-checked)

- **Item pool:** International Personality Item Pool (IPIP) — public domain (no permission/fee). https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm
- **Instrument:** IPIP-NEO-120. **Citation:** Johnson, J. A. (2014). Development of the IPIP-NEO-120. *Journal of Research in Personality, 51*, 78-89.
- **Verbatim items cross-checked across two independent public-domain repositories:**
  - Alheimsins/b5-johnson-120-ipip-neo-pi-r (data/en/questions.json) — provides text + keyed + domain + facet.
  - NeuroQuestAi/five-factor-e (data/IPIP-NEO/120/questions.json) — independent item list.
  - 119/120 items identical; **1 one-word variant** (item O6 below): Alheimsins "…no absolute right **and** wrong" vs NeuroQuest "…right **or** wrong". This file uses the canonical IPIP wording ("and"). _Confirm against primary source in content-fidelity check._
  - Sampled items also matched ipip.ori.org/30FacetNEO-PI-RItems.htm.

## Scoring

5-point: Very Inaccurate(0) … Very Accurate(4). Reverse-keyed items (keyed = minus) are flipped. Each facet = sum of its 4 items (range 0–16); each domain = sum of its 6 facets (range 0–96). Criterion-referenced bands low/balanced/high.

## Items (verbatim, 120) — grouped by domain → facet, with keying

### Neuroticism (N)

**N1 Anxiety**

- (+) Worry about things
- (+) Fear for the worst
- (+) Am afraid of many things
- (+) Get stressed out easily

**N2 Anger**

- (+) Get angry easily
- (+) Get irritated easily
- (+) Lose my temper
- (−) Am not easily annoyed

**N3 Depression**

- (+) Often feel blue
- (+) Dislike myself
- (+) Am often down in the dumps
- (−) Feel comfortable with myself

**N4 Self-Consciousness**

- (+) Find it difficult to approach others
- (+) Am afraid to draw attention to myself
- (+) Only feel comfortable with friends
- (−) Am not bothered by difficult social situations

**N5 Immoderation**

- (+) Go on binges
- (−) Rarely overindulge
- (−) Easily resist temptations
- (−) Am able to control my cravings

**N6 Vulnerability**

- (+) Panic easily
- (+) Become overwhelmed by events
- (+) Feel that I'm unable to deal with things
- (−) Remain calm under pressure

**E2 Gregariousness**

**E3 Assertiveness**

- (+) Take charge
- (+) Try to lead others

**E4 Activity Level**

**E5 Excitement-Seeking**

- (+) Love excitement

**E6 Cheerfulness**

- (+) Radiate joy

### Openness to Experience (O)

**O1 Imagination**

- (+) Have a vivid imagination
- (+) Enjoy wild flights of fantasy
- (+) Love to daydream

**O2 Artistic Interests**

- (+) Believe in the importance of art
- (+) See beauty in things that others might not notice

- (−) Do not enjoy going to art museums

**O3 Emotionality**

- (+) Experience my emotions intensely
- (+) Feel others' emotions
- (−) Rarely notice my emotional reactions
- (−) Don't understand people who get emotional

**O4 Adventurousness**

- (+) Prefer variety to routine
- (−) Prefer to stick with things that I know
- (−) Dislike changes
- (−) Am attached to conventional ways

**O5 Intellect**

- (+) Love to read challenging material
- (−) Avoid philosophical discussions
- (−) Have difficulty understanding abstract ideas
- (−) Am not interested in theoretical discussions

**O6 Liberalism**

- (+) Tend to vote for liberal political candidates
- (+) Believe that there is no absolute right and wrong
- (−) Tend to vote for conservative political candidates
- (−) Believe that we should be tough on crime

### Agreeableness (A)

**A1 Trust**

- (+) Trust others
- (+) Believe that others have good intentions
- (+) Trust what people say
- (−) Distrust people

**A2 Morality**

- (−) Use others for my own ends
- (−) Cheat to get ahead
- (−) Take advantage of others
- (−) Obstruct others' plans

**A3 Altruism**

- (+) Love to help others
- (+) Am concerned about others

- (−) Take no time for others

**A4 Cooperation**

- (−) Love a good fight
- (−) Yell at people
- (−) Insult people
- (−) Get back at others

**A5 Modesty**

- (−) Believe that I am better than others
- (−) Think highly of myself

**A6 Sympathy**

- (+) Sympathize with the homeless
- (+) Feel sympathy for those who are worse off than myself
- (−) Am not interested in other people's problems
- (−) Try not to think about the needy

### Conscientiousness (C)

**C1 Self-Efficacy**

- (+) Complete tasks successfully
- (+) Excel in what I do

**C2 Orderliness**

- (+) Like to tidy up

- (−) Leave my belongings around

**C3 Dutifulness**

- (+) Keep my promises
- (+) Tell the truth
- (−) Break rules
- (−) Break my promises

**C4 Achievement-Striving**

**C6 Cautiousness**

- (−) Rush into things
