How to use it

The value is not in collecting labels. It is in noticing, testing, and revisiting your own interpretation.

Step 01

Answer honestly

For self-reports, choose what is usually true. For reasoning puzzles, work independently and give each problem your best attempt.

Step 02

Question the result

Keep what fits, challenge what does not, and write your own meaning in the reflection.

Step 03

Return later

Future check-ins make change visible. Compare the context too: practice, timing, and circumstances can all move a result.
