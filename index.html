How to use it

The value is not in collecting labels. It is in noticing, testing, and revisiting your own interpretation.

Step 01

Answer honestly

Choose what is usually true, not what sounds most impressive. There are no right answers.

Step 02

Question the result

Keep what fits, challenge what does not, and write your own meaning in the reflection.

Step 03

Return later

Save your result file. Future check-ins make change visible without pretending you are fixed.
